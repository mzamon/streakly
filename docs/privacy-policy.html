Streakly privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Streakly ("the app") is a habit-tracking app created as a student project at The Independent Institute of Education. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account data: your name, e-mail address and (if you use Google sign-in) your Google profile name and photo. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
  • App data you create: habits, completion history, notes, rewards and redemptions.
  • Optional profile data: a profile photo (stored in Firebase Storage), phone number and date of birth (stored only on your device).
  • Device data for notifications: a Firebase Cloud Messaging token, your language and your time zone.

Why

To sign you in, keep your habits in sync between your devices, send reminders and streak alerts, and show your progress. We do not sell data, show ads or share data with third parties other than Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Messaging) and our API host (Render), which process data on our behalf.

Storage and security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Cloud data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage under your account id; only you can access it through the app. A copy of your habits is kept on your phone so the app works offline.

Your choices

  • Export your data (CSV or JSON) in Settings ▸ Data & backup.
  • Delete all data, or your whole account, in Settings. Account deletion removes your Firebase account and all cloud data.
  • Turn notifications off in Settings ▸ Notifications or in your phone's settings.

Children

Streakly is not directed at children under 13.
